📞 On the call
  • The packs are your thermometer. Any ice left means it stayed cold, however late it was.
  • Ask when it went in the fridge. Hours on the porch matter more than days in transit.
  • Say what you checked, then the answer. Your packs still had ice, and I checked the weather at your address for the days it was delayed. This medication handles far more than that. It's fine to use.
  • Close call? Weigh a weaker dose against a missed dose. A mild excursion costs potency, not safety. Skipping a dose is often the bigger risk.
  • Still unsure? Call the manufacturer's medical information line with the facts from Copy summary. They answer from their own stability data, usually the same day.
  • Anxious patient? Give a safety net. If anything looks different from your last dose, or you'd just feel better with a fresh one, call me and we'll replace it.
  • Paste the summary into the record. Every call logged is data for next season.
