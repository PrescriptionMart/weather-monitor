A normal one-day transit is covered by the pack-out's gel packs and isn't counted. For delayed days beyond that, we don't know how long the product was actually warm, so the check assumes the worst case: outdoor air the whole time. Observations go back about a week.
3 · Observed ambient temperature
These are outdoor air temperatures at the nearest weather station, not the temperature inside the package, and we usually don't know how long the product was actually exposed — so the hour counts are a worst-case upper bound (outdoor air the whole window). Conditioned gel packs and an insulated shipper keep it cooler; a delivery van or trailer in the sun can run hotter. Evidence for the pharmacist's judgment, not the judgment itself.
