A delivery has two independent tests: was it out of the fridge too long, and did it get too warm. This page answers the first one, for every product, from the labelled allowance on our own stability sheet. It needs no ZIP and no weather. For the second, use the Excursion Check.

Duration only. A product that clears this page can still fail on temperature, and one that fails here is a manufacturer call regardless of how cool it stayed. Assumes a normal next-day transit and our 48-hour pack-out, so the ship day and the day after are covered and the delay is what gets scored. Products marked banded publish more than one window (Aimovig allows 7 days up to 77°F and, separately, 2 days at 86-104°F); the figure here is the primary window, and the Excursion Check picks the band the actual peak falls in.
