Ship night
Pick the night the shipment goes out. It's delivered around midday the next day and we account for a few hours on the porch. One rule: WINTER PACK if the coldest hour between 11am and 6pm on delivery day is ≤35°F at any town we actually ship to in that state; otherwise standard. Generic cold-corner towns can't flip a state on their own — if one is cold, it's listed as a cold-pocket exception (winter pack only if the label is going there). The overnight low is shown for context only — a package left out past dark is outside our accountable window.
